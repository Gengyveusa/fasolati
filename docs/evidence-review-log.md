# Fasolati evidence review log

This log is the human release gate for future claim changes. It is deliberately separate from the ledger's editorial source-check dates; an automated check must not be represented as approval by a scientist or clinician.

## Current candidate

The candidate ledger organizes the selected science map, two clinical-review summaries, one measurement-design entry and two product-design boundaries. Historical revision entries summarize wording corrections from the prior quality release. They do not claim a new clinical study or a newly completed review by a named external expert.

The next-phase candidate itself remains pending Thad's release decision. No new human reviewer sign-off is asserted here.

## Record format

For each future change, append a completed record containing:

- Claim ID and title.
- Source URL, source version or publication date, and date actually checked.
- Relevant experimental setting, population, route or dose, comparator and uncertainty.
- Previous wording and proposed wording.
- Reason for the change and interpretation limit.
- Name and role of the human reviewer.
- Review decision: accepted, narrowed, rejected, or needs more evidence.
- Actual review date.
- Release approval and repository commit or pull request.
- Whether the public page, JSON export and RSS entry were updated together.

An incomplete record means the proposed change is not yet approved for release. Keep rejected proposals and the reason for rejection; the record should show narrowing as clearly as expansion.
