# Loria evidence checklist

**Fasolati · Product-information questions · Version 01 · September 13, 2026**

Use this checklist to request a current product record. It is not a label, use instruction, purchase offer or clinical recommendation.

## Start with the exact product

Fasolati publishes the name “Loria™ Daily Oral Rinse” and asks readers to request current formulation, labeling, purchasing information and finished-formulation evidence. This is Fasolati’s own description, not independent verification. [Fasolati product information](https://fasolati.life/loria/)

- [ ] Which exact Loria product, SKU, pack size and market does the response cover?
- [ ] Who is the responsible manufacturer or supplier?
- [ ] What are the label and formula revision identifiers and effective dates?
- [ ] Can the supplier provide the complete, current front and back labels—not just marketing copy?
- [ ] Are the photographs of this exact Loria-labeled product? Who owns the images, and when were they taken?

**Record:** Product/SKU: __________ · Market: __________ · Version/date: __________

## Formulation and use information

- [ ] What is the complete ingredient declaration, including active and inactive ingredients where applicable?
- [ ] Which concentrations are relevant to the stated intended use, and where are they documented?
- [ ] What do the actual label directions say about amount, frequency, duration and administration?
- [ ] What warnings, age limits, contraindications, allergy information or other restrictions appear on that label?
- [ ] What are the storage conditions, expiry and after-opening requirements?
- [ ] Who can answer questions about the applicable label without supplying individualized medical advice through a general inquiry form?

Do not infer directions or suitability from the words “Daily Oral Rinse.” This checklist intentionally provides no dose or use schedule.

## Evidence that matches the claim

- [ ] What exact product-specific claim is being made? Copy the wording.
- [ ] Is the support a finished-product study, an ingredient study, a laboratory experiment or biological background?
- [ ] Was the tested finished formulation identical to the version being discussed? Where is that identity documented?
- [ ] Is the full report available, rather than a summary, testimonial or reference list?
- [ ] What population, sample size, comparator, endpoints and follow-up were used?
- [ ] Were outcomes prespecified? What are the absolute results, uncertainty and missing-data limitations?
- [ ] What adverse events, tolerability findings and limitations were reported?
- [ ] Who funded and conducted the work, and what conflicts were disclosed?
- [ ] Does the claim remain within the studied use, population, formulation and endpoint?
- [ ] Is an oral outcome being incorrectly presented as evidence of a systemic-health benefit?

**Evidence record:** Claim: __________ · Full-report URL: __________ · Tested version: __________

Ingredient literature should remain separate from finished-product evidence. A plausible mechanism is not a substitute for a report about the product actually supplied.

[Gengyve product information](https://gengyveusa.com/products/gengyve) · [Loria product information](https://fasolati.life/loria/)

- [ ] If Gengyve information is offered as Loria support, is there a signed, dated manufacturer statement linking the exact products, formula versions and markets?
- [ ] Does that statement address labeling and evidence applicability, not just a shared company or similar ingredients?
- [ ] Are photographs, reviews, directions and endorsements still attached to the product they actually describe?

## Market and purchasing questions

- [ ] What regulatory classification and market-specific records support the supplier’s description? Do not infer approval from a company name, registration or listing.
- [ ] What availability, ordering route, price, shipping, returns and recurring-payment terms apply now? Request a dated answer.
- [ ] Is the conversation an information inquiry, an offer or an actual order? Confirm before proceeding.

No availability, price, clinical benefit or FDA status is represented by this checklist.
